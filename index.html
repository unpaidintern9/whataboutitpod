About Susan

Susan is a cohost and a huge part of What About It? Coming from the Amish community, she brings her own background and perspective to the conversations she shares with Morgan.

She recently found her way back to the church and has been growing in her faith in Jesus. That journey is part of the story she brings to the podcast as she and Morgan talk about faith, life, and the questions along the way.
